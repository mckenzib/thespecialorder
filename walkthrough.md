# Walkthrough - World 3 Update

I have successfully added **World 3: The Steamer** to the game! This update introduces new platforming mechanics and a unique boss battle.

## New Features

### 1. Cloud Platforms (`=`)
- **Visual**: White cloud emoji `☁️`.
- **Mechanic**: You can jump up through them from below, but they are solid when you land on top. This allows for more vertical fluidity.

### 2. Moving Platforms (`-` / `|`)
- **Visual**: Moving Platform emoji `🛹` (Skateboard/Platform).
- **Mechanic**: 
    - **Horizontal (`-`)**: Moves left and right.
    - **Vertical (`|`)**: Moves up and down.
    - **Physics**: If you stand on them, they carry you!

### 3. New Boss: The Dumpling (`D`)
- **Visual**: Dumpling emoji `🥟`.
- **Behavior**: The "Bouncy" Boss. It doesn't just hover; it performs massive jumps to try and crush you from above.

## Level Changes
- **New Levels**: Levels 9, 10, 11, and 12 (Boss) are now "The Steamer" world.
- **Shifted Levels**: The previous World 3 (Walk-in) starts at Level 13, and World 4 (The Pass) starts at Level 17.
- **Total Levels**: 20.
